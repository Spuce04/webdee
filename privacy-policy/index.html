Privacy Policy

You privacy is valued.

Last Modified

12/08/2025

Who We Are

Webdee is based in the UK and respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Contact: enquiries@webdee.co.uk

What We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Job description / brief for your website

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Provide quotes or proposals
  • Keep a record of our communications

We do not sell your data. We only share it with essential service providers that help us run our site (see below).

Where Your Data Is Stored

Form submissions are stored:

  • In our email inbox
  • In our Formspree account (used to process and deliver form submissions)

Formspree acts as our data processor and handles your data on our behalf. We recommend reviewing Formspree’s privacy information for more details.

How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain enquiry records indefinitely unless you ask us to delete them.

Legal Bases for Processing

We process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests: to respond to your enquiry and manage prospective client relationships.
  • Consent: where you’ve explicitly asked us to contact you or similar.

Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You can:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise any of these rights, email enquiries@webdee.co.uk.

Children’s Data

Our website is not intended for children, and we don’t knowingly collect children’s data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We’ll post any changes on this page and update the “last modified” date above.
