Express Heating & Plumbing

Visit Website

Overview

Express Heating & Plumbing needed a professional, modern online presence to reach more customers and showcase their services.
I designed and built a fully responsive website from the ground up, ensuring it works seamlessly across devices.
The site highlights the company’s offerings, past work, and contact options, helping the team engage with potential clients more effectively.

Key Features

  • Multi-page layout including Projects, Gallery, Services, About, and Contact.
  • Project showcase for detailed descriptions of work they’ve done for different kinds of clients.
  • Custom gallery to showcase highlights of work with clean, high-resolution imagery.
  • Responsive design optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile viewing.
  • Contact form with validation for easy customer inquiries.
  • SEO-friendly structure to improve discoverability in search engines.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Presenting large, detailed images without slowing down page load times.
Solution: Implemented image optimization and lazy loading to maintain speed.

Challenge: Ensuring brand consistency across all pages.
Solution: Developed a reusable design system with consistent typography and color schemes.

Outcome

The finished website provided Express Heating & Plumbing with a polished digital presence, enabling them to attract more inquiries and highlight their expertise.
