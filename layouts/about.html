Our Story

Co-founders of the well renowned Cheesy Toast Shack in St. Andrews, Sam and Kate, alongside with business partners Casey and Andy decided to bring their passion for quality, fresh, and locally sourced food to Dundee. Thus, Loom Deli was born in October 2025 with the vision of creating a vibrant community hub where people can come together to enjoy delicious food with great company.

Environment is Everything

A great meal is more than just the food on your plate, it's about the entire experience. That's why we've created a warm and welcoming atmosphere where you can relax, unwind, and enjoy delicious food made from the freshest ingredients.

Locally Sourced

We are committed to supporting local suppliers. By sourcing our ingredients locally, we ensure that our selection is not only fresh and flavorful but also contributes to the sustainability of Dundee's food community.

Pet Friendly

Pets are part of the family, which is why we welcome all furry friends at Loom Deli. Our pet-friendly environment ensures that both you and your pets can enjoy a comfortable dining experience together. If you have a dog, we're certain they'd enjoy a tasty puppaccino on us!
