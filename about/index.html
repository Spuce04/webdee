About

All about me and my work.

Who I Am

From Dundee, I am a university student studying programming and computing at Abertay University. I love using the creativity, development, and marketing skills I’ve obtained throughout my years of experience to help small businesses, freelancers, and creatives get good results. I value fairness, trust, and communication.

Why Choose Me

Solo Developer

No layers, no handoffs—you work directly with the person building your site. One point of contact, consistent quality, quick decisions.

Local Work

Dundee-based. Happy to meet in person or jump on a quick call—plus I understand the local market and audience.

Fast Turnaround

Lean process and tight feedback loops means I ship fast—without cutting corners. Clear milestones, frequent check-ins, and on-time delivery.

About My Process

Simple, transparent, and collaborative: we start with a quick chat to define goals, audience, and success metrics. I follow up with a mini-proposal (scope, timeline, fixed price). Then we map content and wireframes to nail the structure, and iterate quickly with your feedback. Once approved, I start building your website. You’ll get a staging link to review progress any time. Before launch we test on real devices, hook up forms/analytics, sort domain/hosting, and finalise the content. After launch, I'm available for tweaks or ongoing support.
